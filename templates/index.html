Per transaction: if service charge is enabled, net investment = client payment ÷ [1 + (service charge rate × (1 + SST rate))]; otherwise net investment = client payment. Total charges are the sum of charges on enabled transactions only. Units = each transaction’s net investment ÷ its purchase NAV. Average cost per unit = total net investment ÷ total units. Profit / Loss = current value − total net investment. Return = Profit / Loss ÷ total net investment. Other fees and platform rounding adjustments are excluded.
